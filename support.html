Support

Last updated October 26, 2026

Need help? Email info@noroventure.com and a person will reply, usually within two business days. Include your username and what device you are using.

Account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, progress, practice portfolio, leaderboard entry, posts and comments, and your sign-in. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first, to confirm it is you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription, because Apple bills it. Cancel it first, as described below.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. We will email you a link to choose a new one. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

Can I change my username or display name?

Your display name can be changed any time under Account, and your past posts update to match. Your username is permanent once set, so other people can always tell who wrote a post.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel InvestMatch Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose InvestMatch Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but Pro is not unlocked, or I got a new phone.

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore, signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How does the student price work?

Choose the Student plan and verify a school email address with an academic domain, such as .edu, .ac.uk, .edu.au, .ac.nz, .edu.sg, .ac.in, .edu.ph or .ac.za. We send a link to that address; once you open it, the student price unlocks. Schools whose addresses use other domains cannot be verified yet.

Community

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap the ••• menu on any post or comment by someone else and choose Report or Block. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break the community rules. Blocked people are listed under Account, Blocked users, where you can unblock them.

The simulator

Is any of this real money?

No. Every trade, holding and return in InvestMatch is simulated. Prices are real market data, but nothing can be bought or sold for real, and nothing in the app is financial advice.

A price or number looks wrong.

Market data comes from third parties and can be delayed or wrong. Send the ticker and a screenshot to info@noroventure.com and we will check it. See Data Sources for where each number comes from.
