Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version. InvestMatch is an educational investing simulator. We collect the account details you give us and the activity needed to run the app. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app never touches real money or brokerage accounts.

Who we are

InvestMatch is operated by Malia Noro Ventures LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, United States. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Malia Noro Ventures LLC, and "you" means the person using the app.

What other users can see

Your display name, username, posts, comments and leaderboard entry (total XP and simulated return) are visible to other signed-in users. Your email address, quiz answers, practice portfolio and everything else in the table above are private to you.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check) hosts your account and data and runs the app's server logic, on Google Cloud in the United States.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports.
  • Google Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic, writes the investor briefs and explanations in the app. Requests include stock data and, for portfolio insights, your investor type and the tickers you hold. They do not include your email address, name or username.
  • Finnhub supplies market data and news. Requests are for stock symbols, not for information about you.
  • Apple distributes the app and processes subscription payments.

How we use information

To operate and personalise the simulator, keep your progress, run the community and leaderboard, review reports, apply daily limits, verify student eligibility, fix problems, communicate with you about the service, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights or safety of users or the public, or in connection with a merger or acquisition, in which case we will give notice before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

Children and teens

InvestMatch is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes their child has given us information may write to info@noroventure.com and we will remove it.

Users between 13 and 17 should review this policy with a parent or guardian. No part of the app can move real money.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account, Delete account. That immediately deletes your profile, progress, practice portfolio, leaderboard entry, posts, comments and sign-in, and frees your username. Reports you made about other people's content are kept for moderation records. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name in the app, and request a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by writing to info@noroventure.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR, including the right to object to certain processing and to complain to a supervisory authority. We honour these requests wherever you live.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted by authentication and security rules, and the parts of the app that matter, such as trades and student verification, are checked on our servers rather than trusted from the device. No system is perfectly secure, but the app holds no money and no banking credentials, which limits what a breach could expose.

International users

We operate in the United States, and your information is processed there.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
