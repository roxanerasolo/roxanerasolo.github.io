Why this page exists. InvestMatch grades you against what the market actually did, so it only works if you can see where the numbers come from and how far to trust them.

What the numbers do not include

  • Dividends. Simulated returns are price-only, so a dividend-paying stock looks worse in the app than its real total return.
  • Fees, spreads and taxes. A real position carries commissions or spreads and a tax bill. The simulator models none of them, which makes simulated results look better than reality.
  • Slippage and timing. The simulator assumes you buy at the quoted price. Real orders do not always fill there.

How we handle errors

If a provider returns bad or missing data, the app may show older figures or skip a card. If a number looks wrong, send the ticker and a screenshot to info@noroventure.com and we will check it against the provider.

Limits on use

Market data in InvestMatch is for personal, educational use inside the app. You may not redistribute, resell or republish it. Do not use it to make an actual trading decision, and do not treat a simulated result as a forecast.
